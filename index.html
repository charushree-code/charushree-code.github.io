Research and education

The bookshelf: two published papers, both as lead researcher, and the degrees behind them.

Indoor Violence Detection Using a Lightweight Transformer Model Read on IEEE Xplore

IEEE INCET 2023. Lead researcher on a 1.5-year project

  • Goal: detect violence in home video to support child-safety alerts, where indoor footage is scarce and furniture often blocks the view.
  • Built a lightweight video vision transformer (ViViT) in TensorFlow, trained on 1,112 videos I procured and curated.
  • 88.24% accuracy overall and 98% on partly hidden video, beating a published baseline by 11.97%. Preprocessing cut computation about 3×.

Anatomizing Terrorism Read on IJRASET

IJRASET, Vol. 10, Issue IX, September 2022. Lead researcher

  • Goal: find patterns in 50 years of public Global Terrorism Database records (201,183 incidents) to inform prevention research.
  • Cleaned the data from 135 to 41 features and compared k-NN, Random Forest, Bagging, and a neural network.
  • The neural network classified attack type with 94.63% accuracy; Random Forest predicted damage extent with 0.94 accuracy.
